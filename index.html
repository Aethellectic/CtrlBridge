隱私權政策

生效日期 2026-11-13

CtrlBridge（以下稱「本 App」）讓 Android 手機使用 DualSense Wireless Controller、DualSense Edge Wireless Controller、Xbox Wireless Controller 等手把的進階功能。本政策說明本 App 會使用哪些資料、用途，以及這些資料如何處理。

本 App 沒有帳號系統，也沒有自己的伺服器。手把資料、擷取的聲音和設定都只在你的手機上處理，不會由本 App 收集、上傳、出售或分享。

Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

CtrlBridge ("the app") lets Android phones use advanced features of controllers such as the DualSense Wireless Controller, DualSense Edge Wireless Controller, and Xbox Wireless Controller. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and how it is handled.

The app has no accounts and no servers of its own. Controller data, captured audio, and settings are processed only on your phone and are not collected, uploaded, sold, or shared by the app.

Permissions

  • Audio recording and screen capture: used only when you turn on audio-to-haptics, audio routing, or the light bar's audio effect, to capture audio played by other apps. The microphone is not used to record your surroundings.
  • Accessibility service: used only in Bluetooth mode when you turn it on in system settings. The app explains this and asks for your consent first.
  • USB and Bluetooth: communicating with the controller.
  • Notifications: showing connection status and remaining time.
  • Shizuku (optional): Game Bridge uses Shizuku to create a virtual controller on the phone so games can read the controller. It works only on the phone.

2Third-party services

The app uses the following Google services, which handle data under their own policies. The app cannot access personal data from them.

  • Google AdMob (ads): when you watch a rewarded ad, Google may use the advertising ID, device information, IP address, and similar data to serve and measure ads. In the EEA, the UK, and other applicable regions, the app asks for your consent first. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads. See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Google Play Billing (subscriptions and purchases): payments are handled by Google Play. The app only receives whether a purchase is valid and never receives your payment details.

3Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
